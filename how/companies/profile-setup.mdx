### Team Chemistry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the process by clicking on the Get Started button beside 'Build Team Chemistry'">
    ![Comp Onboarding1 Pn](/images/getting-started.png)

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        In the Team Chemistry page start to create a Team e.g. Engineering or Finance etc.

        <Tip>
          Don't forget to connect your Slack or Teams BEFORE you invite your team members as that will give you the correct channel to invite them to take part in the interview process.
        </Tip>
      </Step>
      <Step>
        Invite your Team Members and select the Channel you want them to complete their interview through. There are 3 options

        - Slack
        - Teams
        - Palantrix invite
          <Info>
            _If Palantrix invite is selected the employee will be sent a link to log into Palantrix and to conduct a video interview or to just answer the questions in a written form._
          </Info>
      </Step>
      <Step>
        Once the employee submits their interview the Palantrix AI will evaluate them against the 20 personality traits and start to build a Team DNA profile for your team.

        The more team members you get to fill in the interview the more robust and complete the profile will be.

        <Frame>
          ![Team Chemistry1](/images/team-chemistry1.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step>
        Once you have received as many of your employee interviews as you want then you can click on the View Team Analysis. This is where the AI aggregates the trait profiles and creates a team profile.

        You will use this profile to screen candidates for future positions in that team - this is outlined in the Post a Job section.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Security - MFA

<img
  src="/images/security1.png"
  alt="Security1 Pn"
  title="Security1 Pn"
  style={{ width:"67%" }}
/>

- Navigate to your Settings from the pull down list in the left navigation panel.
- Click on Settings \> Enable MFA (Multi Factor Authentication).

<img
  src="/images/security2.png"
  alt="Security2 Pn"
  title="Security2 Pn"
  style={{ width:"63%" }}
/>

- Open an authenticator app on your phone - two top choices are Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
- Scan the image and enter in the 6 digit code and click verify.
- All subsequent logins will now require the use of the MFA in order to access your Palantrix account.

<Check>
  Enabling MFA is recommended on your Palantrix account and is a great additional way to keep it secure.
</Check>
