Palantrix helps organisations prepare for, measure and win high value business conversations. Connect your CRM and Palantrix builds Client Digital Twins of the people your team meets, so staff can practise a sales presentation, discovery call or client briefing against that specific customer and get feedback straight away.

After a real conversation takes place, Palantrix follows up in Slack or Microsoft Teams to capture what was asked and which objections came up. That feeds into the next round of preparation, so the platform gets sharper with every conversation your team has.
